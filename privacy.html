Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Blacktide Software LLC ("Blacktide," "we," "us") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to blacktidesoftware.com, to email and phone conversations with us, and to text messages we send.

Information we collect

We only collect information you give us or that we need to respond to you:

  • Contact details such as your name, business name, email address and phone number, when you email, call, text or otherwise reach out to us.
  • Messages and project details you send us, including the content of emails, voicemails and text messages.
  • Basic website data collected automatically by our website host, such as your browser type, pages visited and general location based on IP address. Our host may use cookies to keep the site working and to measure traffic.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your questions and requests.
  • To prepare quotes, deliver projects and provide support.
  • To send text messages you have agreed to receive (see below).
  • To keep records required for billing, taxes and legal obligations.
  • To keep our website and services secure and working properly.

We do not sell your personal information.

Text messaging

If you give us your mobile number and agree to receive text messages, we use that number only to send the messages you agreed to, such as replies to your inquiry, follow-ups on missed calls, and project or appointment updates. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages, or HELP for help.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except service providers that help us deliver messages (such as our messaging provider).

Who we share information with

We share information only when needed to run our business, and only with:

  • Service providers that work on our behalf, such as our website host, email provider and text messaging provider. They may use your information only to provide their service to us.
  • Authorities, when required by law, subpoena or court order, or to protect our rights or someone's safety.
  • A buyer or successor, if our business is sold or merged. This policy would continue to apply to your information.

How long we keep information

We keep information as long as we need it to respond to you, complete work for you, or meet legal and tax record requirements. After that we delete it or keep it in a form that no longer identifies you.

How we protect information

We use reasonable safeguards, such as access controls and encrypted connections, to protect your information. No method of storing or sending data is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

  • You can ask us to show, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you.
  • You can stop text messages at any time by replying STOP.
  • You can ask us to stop emailing you at any time.

To make a request, email captain@blacktidesoftware.com.

Children

Our website and services are meant for businesses and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top of this page.
